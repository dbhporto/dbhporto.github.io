Between the Atlantic and the Mediterranean

At Debrais Boutique Hotel, there is a cuisine that begins in the Atlantic.

It begins in the sea and on the land, in the seasons, in the produce and in the hands of those who know where it comes from. A cuisine that looks to Porto and its surroundings to build, from them, an identity of its own.

Ramón Freixa Porto is born from the meeting of the Atlantic and the Mediterranean: two ways of understanding produce, landscape and culinary culture that find in Porto a new space for dialogue.

Awarded two Michelin stars at Ramón Freixa Atelier in Madrid, Ramón Freixa sees cooking as an ongoing dialogue between tradition and the avant-garde, memory and creativity, produce, technique and emotion.

In Porto, that language meets the Atlantic. Its fish and seafood, the produce of the land, the seasons and the Portuguese larder enter into dialogue with Ramón Freixa’s Mediterranean perspective, giving rise to a menu conceived specifically for this restaurant.

A contemporary, cosmopolitan cuisine in which the Atlantic and the Mediterranean neither clash nor overlap: they meet. Portugal’s land and sea are the starting point; Ramón’s vision is what interprets them and carries them towards a new form of expression.

This vision takes shape every day in the hands of chef Jorge Bolito. Portuguese and a native of Coimbra, his knowledge of the region, its produce and its culinary memory helps bring the Ramón Freixa Porto concept to life.

In the kitchen and in the dining room, the team shares the same high standards: care for the produce, for detail and for every gesture, so that each service becomes an experience that lingers long after the meal.

The Atlantic is the origin.
The Mediterranean, the perspective.
Porto, the place where they meet.
